# Guard Duty — Complete Notes (Roman English)

> Simple Roman English notes for study, exam revision, and interview preparation. Specific procedures may vary by department and workplace; always follow official orders and local SOPs.

## 1. What is Guard Duty?

Guard duty ka matlab hai kisi building, office, gate, camp, sensitive area ya property ki hifazat karna. Guard ka main kaam security maintain karna, unauthorized logon ko rokna, aur suspicious activity ko report karna hota hai.

## 2. Objectives of Guard Duty

1. **Protection:** Logon, property aur government assets ki hifazat karna.
2. **Prevention:** Theft, unauthorized entry aur security violations ko rokna.
3. **Observation:** Assigned area mein hone wali activities par nazar rakhna.
4. **Access Control:** Sirf authorized logon ko entry dena.
5. **Reporting:** Unusual activity ya incident ki report senior officer ko dena.
6. **Emergency Response:** Emergency mein prescribed procedure follow karna.

## 3. Duties and Responsibilities of a Guard

- Duty par waqt se pohanchna.
- Uniform aur assigned equipment ko proper condition mein rakhna.
- Duty orders aur instructions ko samajhna.
- Entry aur exit records maintain karna.
- Visitors ki identity aur authorization verify karna.
- Suspicious activity ko observe aur report karna.
- Duty area ko secure rakhna.
- Apni post ko permission ke baghair na chhorna.
- Confidential information ko protect karna.
- Duty complete hone par proper handover dena.

## 4. Qualities of a Good Guard

- **Alertness:** Hoshiyar rehna.
- **Honesty:** Imandari.
- **Discipline:** Qawaid ki pabandi.
- **Punctuality:** Waqt ki pabandi.
- **Responsibility:** Zimmedari.
- **Patience:** Sabr.
- **Physical fitness:** Jismani fitness.
- **Communication skills:** Wazeh tareeqe se baat karna.
- **Confidentiality:** Sensitive information ko protect karna.

## 5. Types of Guard Duty

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gate Duty | Gate par entry aur exit control karna |
| Static Guard Duty | Ek fixed location ki hifazat karna |
| Patrol Duty | Assigned area mein rounds lagana |
| Access Control Duty | Authorized persons ki entry verify karna |
| Night Guard Duty | Raat ke waqt security maintain karna |
| VIP Security Duty | Authorized VIP protection arrangements mein duty |
| Event Security Duty | Events mein security aur crowd management |
| Perimeter Guard Duty | Building ya compound ki boundary ki hifazat |

## 6. General Rules of Guard Duty

1. Hamesha alert aur attentive rahen.
2. Duty ke waqt mobile phone ya doosri distractions avoid karein.
3. Unauthorized person ko entry na dein.
4. Kisi incident ko hide na karein.
5. Visitors ke saath respectful behaviour rakhein.
6. Duty orders aur official SOPs follow karein.
7. Suspicious item ko touch ya move na karein; safe distance rakhein aur prescribed emergency procedure follow karein.
8. Emergency mein senior officer aur relevant emergency services ko inform karein.
9. Duty post ka proper record maintain karein.
10. Relieving guard ko zaroori information hand over karein.

## 7. Incident Reporting

Incident report karte waqt yeh details deni chahiye:

- **What:** Kya hua?
- **Where:** Kahan hua?
- **When:** Kab hua?
- **Who:** Kaun involved tha, agar maloom ho?
- **Action:** Guard ne kya action liya?
- **Status:** Current situation kya hai?

**Example:**

“Sir, main gate par ek visitor aaya hai jiski authorization verify nahi hui. Main ne site procedure ke mutabiq entry roki hai aur aapki instructions ka intezar kar raha hoon.”

## 8. Handover and Takeover

**Handover:** Duty complete karte waqt next guard ko zaroori information aur responsibility dena.

**Takeover:** Duty start karte waqt post, records, keys ya assigned equipment ko procedure ke mutabiq receive karna.

Handover ke waqt pending incidents, visitor records, equipment ki condition aur relevant instructions communicate karni chahiye.

## 9. Emergency Situations

Emergency mein guard ko:

- Calm rehna chahiye.
- Apni aur doosron ki safety ko priority deni chahiye.
- Alarm ya emergency reporting procedure follow karna chahiye.
- Senior officer ko jaldi inform karna chahiye.
- Fire, medical emergency ya suspicious package ke liye relevant emergency procedure follow karna chahiye.
- Incident ka accurate record maintain karna chahiye.

## 10. Guard Duty Interview Questions

### Q1. What is guard duty?

Guard duty is the responsibility of protecting people, property and premises from unauthorized access, theft and security threats.

### Q2. What is the main duty of a guard?

The main duty of a guard is to maintain security, control access, observe suspicious activities and report incidents.

### Q3. What is access control?

Access control is the process of allowing entry only to authorized persons.

### Q4. What should a guard do if an unauthorized person arrives?

The guard should follow the site's access-control procedure, refuse unauthorized entry and inform the supervisor when required.

### Q5. Why is punctuality important in guard duty?

Punctuality ensures continuous security coverage and proper relief between guards.

### Q6. What is a duty handover?

Duty handover is the transfer of responsibility and important information from one guard to another.

### Q7. What is the difference between observation and reporting?

Observation means noticing an activity, while reporting means communicating relevant information to the responsible person.

### Q8. What should a guard do during an emergency?

The guard should follow emergency procedures, protect life, raise the alarm and notify the appropriate authorities.
